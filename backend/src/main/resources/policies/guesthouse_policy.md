# JKLU MDC Guest House Policy

## Eligibility
- Guest house rooms may only be booked by STAFF or ADMIN.
- Purpose must be official: visiting faculty, conference speaker, examiner, official guest.

## Room Types
- STANDARD (G-101 to G-104, G-303): 1–2 persons, ₹1,000–₹1,200/night.
- DELUXE (G-201, G-202, G-203, G-204, G-304): 2–3 persons, ₹2,000–₹2,200/night.
- SUITE (G-202, G-301, G-302): 3–4 persons, ₹3,500/night, priority for keynote speakers.

## Booking Rules
- Minimum stay: 1 night.
- Check-in: 12:00 noon. Check-out: 11:00 AM.
- Maximum advance booking: 90 days.
- All guest house bookings require ADMIN approval.

## Cancellation
- Cancel > 48 hours before check-in: full refund.
- Cancel 24–48 hours: 50% charge.
- Cancel < 24 hours: full charge.
