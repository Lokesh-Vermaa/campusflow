# JKLU CampusFlow Booking Policy

## Eligibility
- STUDENT: May book standard CLASSROOM venues only. No amphitheatres, labs, or guest rooms.
- STAFF: May book any venue type (CLASSROOM, LAB, AMPHITHEATRE), guest rooms, and parking.
- ADMIN: May book any resource. Admin approvals are required for AMPHITHEATRE and guest room bookings.

## Approval Thresholds
- Bookings for > 50 attendees require ADMIN approval.
- Amphitheatre bookings always require ADMIN approval.
- Guest house bookings always require ADMIN approval.
- Standard classroom bookings (≤ 50 attendees) may be auto-approved.

## Booking Hours
- Campus venues are bookable 08:00 to 20:00 IST.
- Guest rooms: Check-in 12:00, Check-out 11:00.
- Outside these hours, booking is not permitted.

## Cancellation
- Bookings may be cancelled by the owner up to 24 hours before the event.
- Admin may cancel any booking at any time.

## Conflict Resolution
- Timetable slots take priority over all bookings.
- If a room has a timetable conflict, the system suggests alternative rooms of similar capacity.
- If the same slot has a confirmed booking, a 409 Conflict is returned with alternatives.

## Special Rules
- T-102 Networks Research Lab is restricted: not bookable for outside events.
- A-301 Faculty Board Room requires HOD approval (bookable_outside = false).
- M-301 Dean Conference Room is not bookable for outside events.
