# JKLU Venue Policy

## Room Types
- CLASSROOM: Standard lecture/seminar rooms, 25–75 capacity.
- LAB: Computer and research labs, require equipment clearance.
- AMPHITHEATRE: Large open-air or auditorium spaces, > 100 capacity.

## Lab Usage
- Computing Labs (T-101, T-102, T-103, T-302): Require IT department clearance.
- T-102 Networks Research Lab: Restricted — internal use only, not available for outside events.
- Analytics Lab (M-103): Finance and analytics software, STAFF/ADMIN only.

## Amphitheatre Rules
- Main Auditorium (A-201, 120 capacity): Cultural events, conferences, convocations.
- Tech Amphitheatre (T-201, 70 capacity): Tech talks, workshops with live demos.
- Management Amphitheatre (M-201, 150 capacity): Business conferences, alumni events.

## AV Equipment
- Projector: Available in all classrooms and labs.
- Audio System: Available in amphitheatres and select classrooms (A-101, M-104, A-301).
- Smartboard: Available in select rooms (A-101, A-202 equiv, T-201, T-301).
